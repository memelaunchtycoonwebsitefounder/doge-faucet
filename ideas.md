# Doge Faucet — Design Brainstorm

## Approach 1: Crypto-Punk Neon Arcade
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Cyberpunk / Retro Arcade
**Core Principles:**
- Dark background with electric neon accents (cyan, magenta, gold)
- Pixel-art inspired borders and glitch effects
- High contrast, energetic, and playful

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: deep navy/black (#0d0d1a)
- Primary accent: electric yellow-gold (Doge coin color)
- Secondary: neon cyan and hot pink
- Emotional intent: excitement, urgency, fun

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric card layout with scanline overlays
- Central "claim" button as a glowing focal point
- Sidebar leaderboard with scrolling ticker

**Signature Elements:**
- Glitch text animation on the title
- Neon border glow on interactive elements
- Pixelated Shiba Inu mascot

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Button press triggers coin-rain particle animation
- Countdown timer with pulsing neon ring
- Toast notifications styled as retro terminal messages

**Animation:**
- Glitch flicker on title every 8s
- Coin particles burst on claim
- Countdown ring pulses when < 10s

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Press Start 2P" (pixel font)
- Body: "Space Mono" (monospace)
- Hierarchy: 3xl bold title, xl section headers, sm body
</text>

## Approach 3: Premium Crypto Dashboard (Dark Mode)
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Fintech Minimalism / Dark Luxury
**Core Principles:**
- Sophisticated dark UI with gold accents
- Data-forward layout with clean hierarchy
- Professional yet approachable crypto aesthetic

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: rich charcoal (#111827) and dark slate
- Primary: Dogecoin gold (oklch warm gold)
- Accent: soft amber and muted teal

- Smooth ring progress for countdown

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Syne" (geometric, modern)
- Body: "DM Sans" (clean, readable)
- Hierarchy: 5xl bold hero amount, 2xl headers, sm labels
